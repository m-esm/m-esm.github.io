# Personal portfolio revision — 27 October 2026

The previous design overemphasized a generic orbiting sculpture, slogan-sized typography and long sequential sections. This pass makes the owner unmistakable and gives interaction an explanatory purpose.

## Decisions

- Identity first: Mohsen / Moshe, Helsinki, current role, professional background and direct profile links in a compact first screen.
- Replace the abstract globe entirely. A stationary isometric workbench combines a recognizable six-wheel delivery robot, its route and a record/waveform. Focus controls explain Starship versus Songtradr; this is an original conceptual illustration, not a representation of their production systems or a connection between the companies.
- Reduce headline sizes and chapter spacing throughout. Contain the WebGL scene in the hero rather than floating it behind all prose.
- Professional chapters: concise personal contribution first; company imagery supports it. Starship scope controls stay because they explain actual work. Songtradr remains limited to verified role context.
- Projects: searchable/filterable explorer, with selected-project detail and useful miniature interactions. Promote overlooked personal products, retain strong robotics and platform evidence, group smaller tools rather than treating all repositories equally.
- Timeline: compact role rows with expandable context; retain all verified dates and distinct travel stories. Independent work is represented in the project explorer and chronological history.
- Recommendations: retain verified quotations and manual selection, reduce empty space.
- World entrance and contact: short, obvious next actions. The original playable habitat is retained.
- Reading document: same newly researched project material, static summaries and links, smaller headings. Preserve transcripts, licenses and credits.
