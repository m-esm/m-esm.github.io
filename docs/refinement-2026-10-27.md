# Section-by-section refinement

The prior pass had useful content but repeated the same box, eyebrow, explanatory caption and generic miniature-demo pattern. This pass keeps the personal identity and robotics/music subject while making each section do a different job.

Palette: deep marine #101d28, steel #233b49, pale slate #b8cbd1, ink-white #edf2ef, restrained copper #d2a585. IBM Plex Sans for all reading and labels. Compact 38–44px personal heading, 28–32px section headings. Left-aligned copy; no oversized slogans. The 3D workbench remains the main visual signature.

- Navigation/hero: one concise introduction, direct routes to work and projects, quieter controls and less repeated role text. More detailed workbench construction and contrasting object materials.
- Songtradr: current role as a compact editorial panel; contextual company image subordinate to personal scope. Avoid repeating Starship history or suggesting unverified employer deliverables.
- Starship: a proper case study with readable scope and implementation context. The interactive view explains the relationship between API, console, integration tooling and monitoring; each selection has a distinct technical explanation.
- Recommendations: show both sourced quotes. A carousel is unnecessary for two short quotes.
- Projects: real imagery and concrete implementation evidence first. A visual catalog opens complete project sheets. Remove the invented nutrition table and unrelated artillery microgame; keep the explicitly labeled handpan/torque interactions that actually explain a project.
- Career: a connected date rail with a readable selected chapter. Preserve separate travel stories and all dates.
- World entrance: show a real captured view of the playable habitat instead of a generic constellation.
- Contact: direct personal links with practical labels; remove the repeated biography and generic closing slogan.
- Reading: keep the same factual material, align typography/palette and remove redundant explanatory copy.

Avoid adding chrome merely to make something look technical: no arbitrary counters, fake terminal output, fictitious metrics, or production architecture claims. Existing sourced details remain the boundary of the prose.
