# Motion redesign

The homepage becomes a cinematic portfolio; the existing interactive habitat remains available through #/sector/arrival. Verified content stays shared with the reading document.

Palette: abyss #080c20, midnight #171938, pearl #f2f0ff, iris #a79aff, glacial blue #83e8ef, warm light #ffc6a0. IBM Plex Sans provides large sculptural titles and readable body copy; existing local font licenses remain.

One persistent WebGL sculpture ties the story together: luminous orbital ribbons and a faceted pearl core. Its orientation and composition follow scroll and pointer position; motion can be paused. Text is ordinary HTML. Chapters pair professional evidence with editorial imagery rather than identical cards. Personal projects follow professional work. The long timeline becomes an alternating constellation of dated stories.

Critique: a dark gradient and floating cards alone would be generic. The signature is the custom twisting ribbon geometry, deliberate changes in composition between chapters, exceptionally large type, and wide image-led stories. Keep decoration out of body copy and retain a reading alternative if graphics fail.

## Component decisions

- Navigation: a custom ME mark, persistent chapter links, compact mobile menu and a separate playable-world entrance.
- Hero: oversized typography balanced against original iridescent ribbon geometry. The sculpture changes scale with chapters and becomes quieter behind reading content.
- Songtradr: a record-sleeve composition, sculpted vinyl and waveform, using credited company photography without suggesting ownership of subsidiary products.
- Starship: wide real-world photography, a concise architecture story, four selectable 3D scope models and a separately identified partner strip.
- Recommendations: an intentional editorial pause, with a reader-controlled selector and two verified quotations. No automatic carousel.
- Projects: actual Parviz CAD and Mechlib imagery, an explorable agent-routing model, and a WebSocket/Redis explanation instead of interchangeable abstract graphics.
- Career: three navigable eras, with professional and independent tracks, employer identities, dates and individual travel stories.
- World entrance: a spatial destination selector whose controls update the real station route.
- Contact: three large destinations with distinct purposes—conversation, source code and reading.
- Loading, favicon, reading document and station controls: the same indigo/pearl identity, with local assets and a static reading escape hatch.

The persistent sculpture is the main continuous motion. Other motion is tied to input or describes a conceptual data flow; pause and reduced-motion preferences cover automatic animation. Models are illustrations, not live operations or undisclosed production diagrams.
